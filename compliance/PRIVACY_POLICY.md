# Privacy Policy for Mifela POS

## 1. Data Collection
We collect only the data necessary to operate our POS and Inventory system, including:
- Merchant identity (name, email, store details)
- Transaction history (Sales, Inventory, Staff data)

## 2. Data Usage
Data is stored locally on your machine and optionally synced with our encrypted cloud backend for backup and multi-terminal sync.

## 3. Your Rights
Under GDPR/DPDP, you have the right to:
- Access your data
- Request deletion
- Withdraw consent
