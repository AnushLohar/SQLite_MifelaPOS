# Design System

## Palette
- **Primary**: Slate 900 (Text), Indigo 600 (Action)
- **Neutral**: Slate 50 (Bg), Slate 200 (Border)
- **Radius**: 12px (Standard), 8px (Tight)
- **Shadow**: Subtler (ring-1 ring-slate-200) instead of heavy drop-shadows

## Typography
- Inter, 14px base, tight letter-spacing
